Thermal Management Challenges

🔥
Ohmic Heating
I²R heating in cables and connectors scales with current squared. At 500A, even 0.001Ω resistance generates 250W of heat per connection point.
65°C
⚡
Battery Polarization
Fast charging causes concentration gradient buildup. Heat from electrochemical polarization can cause hotspots if cooling is inadequate.
48°C
🔋
SEI Layer Growth
High charging rates accelerate SEI formation. Exothermic reactions can trigger thermal runaway if temperature exceeds 80°C.
72°C
❄️
Cold Temperature Limits
Below 0°C, Li+ plating occurs instead of intercalation. Charging must slow to prevent metallic lithium deposition on anodes.
-15°C

Key Insights

📈
Charging Speed Moore's Law
Charging power has doubled every 2.5 years since 2012. By 2030, 5-minute full charges will be standard, eliminating range anxiety entirely.
🌡️
Thermal Bottleneck
The limiting factor for fast charging is no longer the grid or battery chemistry—it's thermal management. Solving heat dissipation is the key to ultra-fast charging.
🔌
Standardization Convergence
CCS and GB/T are converging toward unified ultra-high power standards. Regional fragmentation is ending as 800V architectures become global.
⚡
Solid-State Revolution
SSB technology will enable 10C+ charging rates with no thermal runaway risk. The combination of sulfide electrolytes and lithium metal anodes transforms fast charging physics.
