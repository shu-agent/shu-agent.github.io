Thermal Physics Equations

Ohmic Heat Generation
Q_gen = I² · R_internal
Joule heating from internal resistance. At 200A discharge with 0.003Ω internal resistance: Q = 200² × 0.003 = 120W per cell.
Heat Diffusion (Fourier's Law)
q = -k · ∇T
Heat flux proportional to temperature gradient. Thermal conductivity k varies: NMC = 1.2 W/(m·K), LFP = 0.9 W/(m·K), Liquid coolant = 0.6 W/(m·K).
Thermal Time Constant
τ = ρ · c_p · V / (h · A)
Time to reach 63% of final temperature. Depends on thermal mass (ρc_pV) and convective surface area (A). Typical values: 500-2000s for EV battery packs.
Arrhenius Temperature Dependence
k = A · exp(-Ea/RT)
Chemical reaction rate acceleration with temperature. Every 10°C increase roughly doubles reaction rates for SEI growth and aging mechanisms.

Cooling System Technologies

💨
Air Cooling
Forced air circulation with fans. Simple, low cost, widely used in entry-level EVs. Limited heat capacity of air reduces effectiveness at high C-rates.
Cooling Rate: 500-1500 W/m²K
Complexity: Low
💧
Liquid Cooling
Water/glycol coolant flowing through channels in battery module. 10x heat capacity of air. Used in most modern EVs (Tesla, Lucid, Rivian).
Cooling Rate: 5000-15000 W/m²K
Complexity: Medium
🌫️
Phase Change Materials
PCM absorbs heat during melting (latent heat ~200 kJ/kg). Maintains near-constant temperature during phase transition. Passive, no pump or fan power needed.
Latent Heat: 200 kJ/kg
Temp Stability: ±0.5°C
⚡
Thermoelectric (Peltier)
Solid-state heat pumps using Peltier effect. Precise temperature control, no moving parts or coolant. Lower efficiency (~10-15%) but excellent for small volumes.
COP: 0.5-1.5
Power Draw: 100-500W

Thermal Runaway Progression

60-70°C
Stage 1: SEI Decomposition Begins
SEI layer starts decomposing above 60°C. Exothermic reaction releases ethylene gas. Internal temperature rising, reversible if cooling is applied.
80-100°C
Stage 2: SEI Collapse & Li Intercalation
SEI loses structural integrity. Exothermic reaction between electrolyte and anode releases heat. Thermal runaway process begins to accelerate. Critical intervention window.
120-150°C
Stage 3: Separator Melting & Internal Short
Polyethylene separator melts at 120°C. Internal soft short develops. Ohmic heating increases dramatically. Chain reaction becomes self-sustaining. Venting may occur.
200-300°C
Stage 4: Cathode Decomposition & Oxygen Release
NMC/NCA cathode releases oxygen at high temperature. Oxygen combines with organic electrolytes, causing rapid combustion. Large quantities of gas generated. Cell housing may rupture.
500°C+
Stage 5: Full Thermal Runaway & Fire
Complete cell failure. Fire propagation to adjacent cells. temperatures can exceed 1000°C. Toxic gases (HF, CO, HCN) released. Requires specialized fire suppression.
