Quantum Tunneling in Li+ Transport

When particles breach barriers that classical physics declares impassable

WKB Approximation

Quantum tunneling probability through a rectangular barrier of width d:

P = exp(-2√(2mφd²)/ħ)

where m = ion mass, φ = barrier height, d = barrier width

Barrier Parameters

SEI layer thickness: 5-100 nm

Barrier height (Li₂O): ~2 eV

Li⁺ tunneling time: ~10⁻¹² s

τ_tunnel ∝ exp(α·d/√φ)

Landauer-Büttiker Formalism

Quantum transport through interfaces treated as scattering problem:

G = (2e²/h) · T(EF)

Conductance quantized in units of 2e²/h

Li⁺ Wave Functions in Crystal Lattices

Lithium ions as quantum mechanical probability distributions

The Time-Independent Schrödinger Equation

Ĥψ = (-ħ²/2m)∇²ψ + V(r)ψ = Eψ

For Li⁺ in a periodic crystal potential V(r) = V(r + R), the wave function satisfies Bloch's theorem:

ψ_k(r) = e^(ik·r) · u_k(r)

Intercalation States

Li⁺ occupy discrete sites in cathode lattice (e.g., octahedral/tetrahedral in NMC):

ψ_i(r) = Σ C_n φ_n(r - R_i)

Wave function expanded in localized basis functions centered at lattice site R_i

Band Formation

As Li⁺ concentration increases, discrete levels merge into bands:

E(k) = E_0 ± γ · cos(k·a)

Tight-binding model: bandwidth 4γ, bandwidth scales with hopping integral γ

Stage Transitions

Li⁺ ordering creates periodic superstructures (Stage 1, 2, 3...):

c_stage = n/(n+1) · c_max

Stage n: Li⁺ fills every n-th gallery; causes voltage plateaus in graphite

Zero-Point Energy

Li⁺ confined to lattice site has residual zero-point energy:

E_ZPE = (ħω₀)/2 = (ħ/2)√(k/m)

~25 meV for Li⁺ in graphite; contributes to entropy and affects voltage

Quantum Coherence in Electrochemical Systems

When quantum superpositions survive long enough to matter

Coherent Transport Regime

At low temperatures, Li⁺ can exhibit coherent tunneling between sites:

P_ii(t) = |〈i|ψ(t)〉|² = cos²(Ωt)

Coherent Rabi oscillation frequency: Ω = γ/ħ (hopping matrix element)

Decoherence Mechanisms

Environment-induced decoherence destroys quantum coherence:

τ_φ ∝ exp(α·T·t/ħ)

Decoherence time τ_φ; at room T, τ_φ ~ 10⁻¹³ s (too short for transport)

Cryo-Electrochemistry

At 77K (liquid N₂), coherent effects become measurable:

T < ΔE/k_B (level spacing)

Cryo-EM studies of SEI: 2-10 nm crystalline domains preserve coherence

Superconducting Analogy

Analogous to Cooper pairs in BCS theory:

ξ = ħv_F/(πΔ)

Coherence length ξ analogous to electrode electronic coherence length

Interface ZPE Effects

At the electrode|electrolyte interface, quantum fluctuations create measurable ZPE contributions:

E_ZPE_interface = (1/2)Σ ħω_i

Sum over all interface vibrational modes; affects differential capacitance

Casimir Force Contribution

Quantum vacuum fluctuations cause attractive force between electrodes:

F_Casimir = -(π²ħc)/(240d⁴) · A

At d < 10 nm, Casimir contributes ~1 MPa attractive force; affects electrode spacing

Research Frontiers

Cutting-edge quantum effects in battery science

Cryo-EM SEI Imaging

At 77K, cryo-electron microscopy reveals SEI nanocrystalline structure (2-10 nm domains) that preserves quantum coherence during imaging, enabling atomic-scale visualization of Li⁺ pathways.

Quantum Transport in SSBs

Solid-state batteries with sulfide electrolytes show non-Arrhenius conductivity at low T, suggesting quantum tunneling contribution to ion transport below 200K.

Spin-Orbit Coupling in Cathodes

NMC cathodes with Ni/Co exhibit spin-orbit coupling affecting Li⁺ site energies; quantum spin states may explain magnetic susceptibility anomalies.

Entanglement in Redox Reactions

Electron transfer in cathodes involves quantum entanglement between electron spins; coherence time ~10 fs enables coherent energy transfer.

Quantum Capacitance

At graphene anodes, quantum capacitance C_q = (dQ/dV)_quantum dominates at low carrier density; directly measures density of states near Fermi level.

Bell's Inequality Tests

Proposed experiments testing non-local correlations in electrochemical systems; violation would indicate quantumness in charge transport beyond classical explanation.
