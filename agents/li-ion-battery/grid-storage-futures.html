Long-Duration Energy Storage (LDES)

Beyond 4-hour storage: emerging technologies for multi-day and seasonal storage to enable 100% renewable grids.

2020-2024
Iron-Air Batteries Enter Commercialization
Form Energy's iron-air technology promises $20/kWh storage cost at 100-hour discharge duration. First commercial projects operational. Grid-scale "rust and air" batteries reversibly oxidize iron to rust.
2023-2027
Carnot-Style Molten Salt Storage
Thermal energy storage using molten salt for 12-24 hour discharge. Malta Inc. and others demonstrate Pumped Heat Energy Storage (PHES). Converts electricity to heat and back with 50%+ efficiency.
2025-2030
Gravity Storage Commercial Scale
Energy Vault's gravity-based system uses stacked concrete blocks lifted by renewable energy. 10-12 hour discharge at utility scale. First commercial plant in China operational.
2027-2035
Liquid Air Energy Storage (LAES)
Highview Power's cryogenic storage liquefies air using off-peak renewable power. Boosts liquid air to drive turbines on demand. 50 MWe systems with 12+ hour duration demonstrated.

Second-Life Battery Applications

EV batteries at 70-80% State of Health still have 10-15 years of useful life for less demanding applications. This emerging market addresses both sustainability and economic opportunity.

🚗➡️🏭

EV → Grid Storage

  • Retired EV packs (70-80% SOH)
  • Stationary storage applications
  • Lower DOD requirements (50-60%)
  • Cost: $30-80/kWh (vs $185 new)
  • Lifecycle: 10-15 years remaining
🏠➡️⚡

Residential Stack

  • Lower performance requirements
  • Shallower cycling (20-30% DOD)
  • Backup and self-consumption
  • Value: $50-100/kWh second-life
  • Enables affordable home storage

Key Insight

By 2030, an estimated 500,000 tonnes of EV battery material will retire annually. Second-life applications can capture 30-40% of the original battery value, while extending useful life and reducing recycling burden. However, careful testing, grading, and warranty management are essential.
