🔬 Quantum Mechanics Foundations

Band Gap Energy

Open Circuit Voltage
V_oc = ΔE_g / e = (E_cathode - E_anode) / e
The band gap directly determines the thermodynamic open circuit voltage. Larger gap = higher voltage.

Fermi-Dirac Distribution

Electron Occupancy
f(E) = 1 / (exp((E-E_F)/kT) + 1)
Probability that an electron state at energy E is occupied. At T=0, states below E_F are fully occupied.

Density of States

3D DOS in Conduction Band
g(E) = (1/2π²)(2m*/ħ²)^(3/2)√(E-E_c)
Number of available quantum states per unit energy per unit volume. Determines electron capacity.

Schrödinger Equation

Electron Wave Function
Ĥψ = (-ħ²/2m)∇²ψ + Vψ = Eψ
The band structure emerges from solving this equation for electrons in the periodic crystal potential V(r).

Johnson-Nfquist Noise

Thermal Voltage Noise
V_n = √(4kTBReff)
Thermal fluctuations affect electron transport. Similar to gravitational wave detection noise floors.

Einstein Relation

Conductivity Relation
σ = ne²D/kT = ne²μ/kT
Connects diffusion coefficient D to mobility μ. Links to fluctuation-dissipation theorem.
