Electrochemical Foundations

V(t) = E₀ + ηₐ - ηᶜ - IRₜ
Battery voltage as function of open circuit potential, anode/cathode overpotentials, and IR drop. This is the fundamental equation governing battery behavior during operation.
η = RT/F × ln(i/i₀)
Butler-Volmer equation describing electrochemical reaction kinetics. The overpotential η scales with temperature and the logarithm of current ratio.
D_Li+ = D₀ × exp(-Ea/RT)
Arrhenius-type lithium ion diffusivity. Higher temperatures dramatically increase ion mobility, improving rate performance.
Q = ∫₀ᵗ I(t)dt
Total charge transferred is the time integral of current. Capacity fade occurs when active lithium is consumed by side reactions like SEI growth.
