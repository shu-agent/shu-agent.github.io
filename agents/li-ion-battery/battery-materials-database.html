Battery Safety Data & Hazards
🔥

Thermal Runaway

Exothermic chain reaction when battery exceeds 130°C. Triggered by: internal short, overcharge, external heat, physical damage.

Stage 1: 80-120°C — SEI decomposition
Stage 2: 120-200°C — Separator melt
Stage 3: >200°C — Cathode decomposition
⚡

Internal Short

Dendrite formation can penetrate separator causing rapid discharge. Creates localized hot spots leading to thermal runaway.

Li plating at >4.25V
Dendrite growth at high C-rate
Physical penetration
💥

Gas Evolution

Electrolyte decomposition releases CO₂, CO, H₂, O₂. Pressure buildup can rupture cell casing. Venting releases toxic & flammable gases.

EC decomposition: CO₂
SEI formation: CH₂=CH₂
Overcharge: CO, H₂, O₂

Safety Mechanisms & Thresholds

Parameter Safe Threshold Warning Level Danger Level Response
Temperature <60°C 60-80°C >80°C Current reduction, cooling
Voltage (per cell) 3.0-4.2V 4.2-4.25V >4.25V Stop charging
Charge Rate <1C 1-2C >2C continuous Thermal monitoring
SOH Warning >80% 70-80% <70% Replace cell

Safety Features in Battery Systems

Battery Management System (BMS)

  • Cell voltage monitoring (per cell)
  • Temperature monitoring (multiple points)
  • Current limiting & protection
  • SOC calculation & balancing
  • Isolation monitoring

Cell-Level Safety

  • CID (Current Interrupt Device)
  • Pressure relief valve
  • Positive temperature coefficient (PTC)
  • Separator shutdown (140°C)
  • Flame-retardant electrolyte
