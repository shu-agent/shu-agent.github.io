Economics

V2G Business Case Analysis

The math behind vehicle-to-grid economics, from individual EV owner returns to aggregator ROI and grid operator savings.

💰

EV Owner Revenue

An average EV with 75 kWh capacity, driven 40 miles/day (leaving 55 kWh available), participating in frequency regulation can earn $150-400/year through aggregator partnerships. Battery degradation cost: ~$50/year (for 500 cycles/year). Net benefit: $100-350/year.
Net Annual Benefit = (Revenue/kW × Available kW) - (Degradation Cost × Cycle Wear)
⚗

Aggregator Margin

Aggregators pool thousands of EVs to meet grid operator minimums (1-10 MW bids). With 10,000 EVs at 10 kW each available: 100 MW capacity. Take rate: 15-25% of the spread between wholesale and ancillary market prices.
Aggregator Margin = (Market Price - Fleet Bid Price) × Dispatch kWh × Take Rate
⚞

Grid Operator Savings

V2G reduces the need for peaker plants ($500-1000/kW capital cost vs $50/kW for EVSE). Every 1 GW of V2G capacity avoids $200M in peaker investments. Additionally, reduced transmission congestion and line loss savings add another $30-80/kW-year.
Avoided Cost = Peaker Capacity ($/kW) × V2G Capacity (kW) + Congestion Savings ($)
🚀

Battery Wear Model

Lithium-ion calendar aging: 2-4%/year regardless of use. Cycle aging is cycling-dependent. V2G duty cycles (20-80% DoD, 1C rate, daily) add ~0.5-1% incremental degradation per year. This is traded off against revenue. Critical: depth-of-discharge management.
Cycle Life Impact = DOD^(0.8) × Current Rate^(0.3) × Temperature Factor × Cycle Count

Technical

Electrochemical & Power Electronics

The hardware and control systems that make bidirectional power flow possible.

⚡

Bidirectional On-Board Charger

Standard AC chargers are unidirectional (OBC). V2G requires bidirectional power conversion: AC-DC (charging) + DC-AC (discharging). Silicon carbide (SiC) and gallium nitride (GaN) semiconductors enable 94-96% round-trip efficiency. Current solutions: 6.6 kW to 22 kW ratings.
🔅

DC Fast Charge V2G

CHAdeMO (Japan) pioneered commercial V2G with the CHAdeMO 2.0 spec (up to 400A, 1.2 kV). CCS (Combined Charging System) is adding V2G in ISO 15118-20. Both use power electronics integrated into the DC charger for bidirectional DC-DC conversion.
🔧

Aggregator Control Platform

Central optimization engine that bids into wholesale markets, receives AGC signals, forecasts fleet availability, and dispatches individual vehicles. Uses mixed-integer programming for unit commitment with stochastic optimization for renewable forecast integration.
🛡

Smart Charging Algorithms

The core challenge: minimize battery degradation while maximizing grid value. Model Predictive Control (MPC) uses 24-hour forecasts to optimally schedule charge/discharge windows. Reinforcement Learning agents are being trained to adapt to real-time grid conditions without explicit programming.
🌊

Fleet State Estimation

At any moment, the aggregator must know available capacity across the fleet: which vehicles are plugged, current SOC, departure time commitments, and preferred minimum charge level. This requires real-time telemetry from every connected vehicle with privacy-preserving aggregation.
💻

Grid Code Compliance

V2G power quality must meet utility interconnection requirements: THD <5%, frequency ride-through (59.5-60.5 Hz for 10+ minutes), anti-islanding protection, power factor correction. Each aggregator must certify compliance with regional grid codes (FERC, EU NC RfG).
Timeline

V2G Technology Roadmap

From early pilots to grid-forming virtual power plants — the evolution of V2G technology.

2012
First V2G Demonstration
Dr. Willett Kempton at University of Delaware demonstrates the first grid-connected EV aggregator with two Ravi EVs, proving the technical concept.
2015
CHAdeMO V2G Standard
CHAdeMO Association publishes the V2G specification, enabling DC fast chargers to bidirectionally flow power. First commercial V2G stations deployed in Japan and Europe.
2018
Aggregators at Scale
Nissan, BMW, and Ford launch V2G pilot programs. The University of Delaware spinout Viricity (now Orix) reaches 1,000 vehicles under management.
2020
ISO 15118-20 Ratified
The international standard for V2G communication over PLC is finalized, enabling CCS-based V2G and Plug & Charge, the foundation for mass adoption.
2022
EU Mandates V2G
EU introduces requirement that all new EVs must be V2G-capable by 2025, and all public chargers above 11 kW must support V2G by 2027.
2024
Ancillary Services Markets Open
FERC Order 2222 takes effect, allowing DER aggregators to participate in wholesale markets. EVs can now directly compete with power plants.
2026
Fleet-Scale V2G Operations
Major automakers integrate native V2G capability. Aggregators manage fleets of 100,000+ vehicles. Vehicle-to-Grid becomes a mainstream grid resource.
2030
Grid-Forming Virtual Power Plant
The aggregated EV fleet operates as a virtual power plant with grid-forming capability — able to establish grid frequency and voltage without any conventional generation. This is the Holy Grail of V2G.
💡

Grid-Forming Mode

When conventional generation fails entirely, the aggregated EV fleet uses advanced power electronics to recreate grid voltage and frequency — forming a microgrid island that can be reconnected when main grid restores.
