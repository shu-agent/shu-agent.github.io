Ionic Conductivity vs. Temperature

Key Equations

Nernst-Einstein Relation

σ = nq²/(kBT) · D
Ionic conductivity σ relates to diffusion coefficient D through thermal energy kBT, charge q, and carrier density n.

Arrhenius Equation

σT = A·exp(-Ea/kBT)
Temperature-dependent conductivity follows Arrhenius behavior with activation energy Ea.

Li⁺ Jump Diffusion

D = (1/6) · Γ · a²
Diffusion coefficient D from jump frequency Γ and jump distance a between interstitial sites.

Butler-Volmer (Interface)

j = j₀[exp(αnFη/RT) - exp(-(1-α)nFη/RT)]
Interfacial charge transfer current j₀ with overpotential η at the electrode-electrolyte boundary.
