📊 Stage 1: Normal Operation

During charging and discharging, lithium ions shuttle between cathode and anode through the electrolyte. The entropy of mixing and internal resistance generate small amounts of reversible heat (Joule heating). Cell temperature rises modestly above ambient.

Q_gen = I²·R_int + T·ΔS·I/F
Joule heating + Entropic heat (reversible)

🔬 Stage 2: SEI Decomposition

The Solid Electrolyte Interphase (SEI) layer, initially stable at 70-80°C, begins decomposing exothermically. This releases stored lithium and exposes fresh anode surface, which reacts with electrolyte solvents (EC, DEC, DMC) in an autocatalytic process.

SEI → Li₂CO₃ + Li₂O + C₂H₄ + heat
Exothermic SEI breakdown (ΔH ≈ -1500 kJ/mol)

⚡ Stage 3: Anode-Electrolyte Reaction

With SEI compromised, exposed lithium metal reacts directly with the organic electrolyte. This reaction generates ethylene gas (C₂H₄), hydrogen (H₂), and methane (CH₄). The pressure inside the cell begins to rise as gases accumulate.

2Li + C₃H₄O₃ → Li₂CO₃ + C₂H₄ + H₂
Li-Electrolyte reaction (exothermic, gas-generating)

💥 Stage 4: Cathode Oxygen Release

At temperatures exceeding 150°C, layered oxide cathodes (LiCoO₂, NMC) begin decomposing and releasing oxygen. This oxygen enables combustion of organic electrolyte vapors, creating a self-sustaining flame front within the cell. The cell voltage begins to collapse.

LiCoO₂ → CoO + Li₂O + ½O₂↑
Cathode decomposition (ΔH ≈ -300 kJ/mol)

⚠️ STAGE 5: THERMAL RUNAWAY

Once core temperature exceeds ~200°C, the exothermic reactions become self-sustaining.
Heat generation rate > heat dissipation rate → Temperature accelerates toward 800°C+
Cell rupture,喷发 flames, and potentially cascading failure to adjacent cells.

⚗️ Thermodynamics of Thermal Runaway

🔥 Heat Generation Sources

The total heat generation rate determines whether a battery can safely dissipate generated heat or whether it will undergo thermal runaway:

Q_total = Q_R + Q_SEI + Q_anode + Q_cathode + Q_electrolyte
Total heat from all exothermic reactions
Q_R (Ohmic) I²·R
Q_SEI 70-120°C onset
Q_anode 100-150°C onset
Q_cathode 150-250°C onset

🌡️ Heat Dissipation

Heat dissipation occurs through convection, conduction, and radiation. The cell's thermal resistance and surface area determine how efficiently it can reject heat.

Qdiss = h·A·(T_core - T_ambient)
Newton's law of cooling (convection)
Qdiss = k·A/L·ΔT
Conduction through cell components

Critical condition for runaway: Q_total > Qdiss at ALL points in the temperature rise.

0°C 50°C 100°C 150°C 200°C 300°C 600°C

📈 Heat Generation vs Temperature Curve

The crossover point (Q_gen = Q_diss) marks the onset of thermal runaway. Before this point, the cell can cool itself. After this point, temperature accelerates uncontrollably.

⚡ Trigger Mechanisms

🔌 External Short Circuit

Direct connection between cathode and anode through external conductive path. Massive current flow causes rapid Joule heating across the entire cell.

P = V²/R_internal
Megawatts possible in milliseconds
  • Damaged cable contact
  • Metal object penetration
  • Vehicle collision

🔋 Internal Short Circuit

Dendrite lithium filaments penetrate the separator, connecting anode to cathode internally. Creates localized hot spot that triggers cascading reactions.

🌡️ External Heat Source

Environmental temperature exceeding SEI decomposition threshold (70-80°C). Common scenarios include fire exposure, solar concentration, or inadequate thermal management.

  • Parking in direct sunlight
  • Proximity to fire source
  • Failed cooling system

⚡ Overcharge

Excessive charging voltage forces lithium plating on anode surface (instead of intercalation). Metallic lithium is highly reactive and triggers electrolyte decomposition.

Li⁺ + e⁻ → Li(s) ↓
Lithium plating (not intercalation)

💨 Rapid Discharge at High Rate

Extreme C-rates (>>2C) cause internal resistance heating that exceeds cooling capacity. The cell cannot dissipate generated heat fast enough, leading to thermal rise.

Q = I²·R·t
Cumulative Joule heating

🔧 Manufacturing Defects

Contamination, electrode misalignment, separator defects, or incomplete formation create inherent vulnerabilities that predispose cells to thermal runaway.

  • Metal particle contamination
  • Wrinkle in separator
  • Insufficient SEI formation

🔥 Flammability Limits

Generated gases accumulate in the cell headspace. When concentration enters the flammable range, any spark or hot spot can ignite the gas, causing cell rupture or external flaming.

Normal Operation

Battery operates within normal parameters. Cell temperature = ambient + modest I²R heating. No exothermic reactions occurring.

Cell-to-Cell Propagation

Adjacent cells experience thermal传播. Failed cell releases heat and flaming debris. Pack-level failure can occur within seconds.

🛡️ Mitigation & Safety Systems

🔧 CID — Current Interrupt Device

A pressure-activated switch that disconnects the cell when internal pressure exceeds safe levels (typically 10-15 PSI). Provides backup protection if primary BMS fails.

🌡️ PTC — Positive Temperature Coefficient

Temperature-dependent resistor that increases resistance dramatically when temperature exceeds threshold. Limits current flow during thermal events.

• Room temp: R ≈ 0Ω
• 80°C: R begins rising
• 120°C: R → kΩ range

🚿 Vent & Pressure Relief

Specially designed vents that rupture at controlled pressure to release gases safely, preventing explosive cell rupture. Critical for directing flames away from personnel.

🔬 Ceramic Separator

High-melting-point ceramic-coated separators maintain integrity at temperatures where conventional polyolefin separators melt and shrink (~130°C).

PP/PE Melting: 130-140°C
Ceramic Stable: >200°C

⚖️ Battery Management System

Electronic system monitoring cell voltage, temperature, current, and SOC. Triggers protective actions (charge halt, discharge halt, isolation) when parameters exceed safe limits.

OVP Threshold: 4.25V
UVP Threshold: 2.7V
OTP Threshold: 55°C
OTC Threshold: -10°C

❄️ Thermal Management System

Active cooling (liquid cooling, refrigeration) or passive cooling (heat sinks, phase change materials) to maintain cell temperature within safe operating windows.

⬤ Liquid Cooling: 5-15 W/K
⬤ PCM: Latent heat absorption
⬤ Heat Pipes: >10 W/K effective
