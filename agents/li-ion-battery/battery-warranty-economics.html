TCO Component Breakdown

Purchase Cost: Pack price ÷ (capacity × usable DOD × cycle life)

O&M Cost: $8-15/kWh/year (monitoring, cooling, repairs)

Replacement Cost: Pro-rated pack cost × probability at year X

Insurance: Tier premium × years × SOH degradation factor

Grid Services: Foregone revenue from second-life applications

Arrhenius Degradation Rate
k = A · exp(-Ea / RT)
Degradation rate constant

A: Pre-exponential factor (s⁻¹)

Ea: Activation energy (kJ/mol)

R: Gas constant (8.314 J/mol·K)

T: Temperature (K)

Ea(NMC) ≈ 25 kJ/mol → Doubles every ~10°C
Ea(LFP) ≈ 20 kJ/mol → Doubles every ~12°C
Weibull Failure Distribution
R(t) = exp(-(t/η)^β)
Reliability function (Weibull)

η (eta): Characteristic lifetime

β (beta): Shape parameter (failure mode)

β < 1: Early-life failures

β = 1: Random/exponential failures

β > 1: Wear-out failures

NMC η=10yr, β=3.5 → 2.3% fail by year 8
LFP η=15yr, β=4.0 → 1.1% fail by year 10
Capacity Fade Model
SOH = 100 · exp(-α·t - β·C)
Exponential fade model

α: Calendar aging coefficient (yr⁻¹)

β: Cycle aging coefficient (cycle⁻¹)

t: Time in years

C: Equivalent full cycles

NMC: α=0.020, β=0.00025
LFP: α=0.010, β=0.00012
Levelized Cost Equation
LCOE = (I + OM + R + Ins) / E_delivered
Levelized Cost of Energy ($/kWh)

I: Capital + replacement (NPV)

OM: Operations & maintenance (NPV)

R: Residual / salvage value

Ins: Insurance premiums (NPV)

E_delivered: Lifetime energy (kWh)

NMC grid storage: LCOE ≈ $0.08-0.12/kWh
LFP grid storage: LCOE ≈ $0.06-0.09/kWh
