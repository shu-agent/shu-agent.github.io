Electrode Structure Types

🟫

Spherical NMC

Secondary particles (5-15 μm) composed of nano-primary particles. Provides high volumetric energy density.

🔷

Platelets LFP

Tabular particles with (010) basal planes exposed. Enables fast Li+ extraction along 2D channels.

📦

Columnar NCA

Rod-like structures with radial grain orientation. Degrades via intragranular cracking.

🧊

Layered LCO

Staged Li ordering in layered structure. Co dissolution at high voltage causes capacity fade.

Real Microscopy Techniques

SEM — Secondary Electron Imaging

Surface topography with ~1nm resolution. Brightness correlates with surface angle (cosine contrast). Essential for particle morphology and fracture analysis.

TEM — Transmission Electron Microscopy

Atomic-scale lattice imaging. FFT patterns reveal crystal structure. STEM-EELS for chemical mapping of Li distribution.

XCT — X-ray Computed Tomography

3D internal structure without sectioning. Resolves electrode cracks, lithium plating, and gas evolution in working batteries.

AFM — Atomic Force Microscopy

Surface mechanical properties at nanoscale. Measures Young's modulus mapping and Li+ diffusion via AFM-IR.
