Phase Transition Fundamentals

🔬 Why Phase Diagrams Matter

Phase diagrams map the thermodynamic stability regions of battery materials, revealing how temperature, state-of-charge, and composition determine crystal structure. Understanding these transitions is critical for predicting capacity fade and designing longer-lasting cathodes.

⚡ Voltage Hysteresis

During phase transitions, batteries exhibit voltage plateaus where multiple phases coexist. This coexistence creates hysteresis losses — energy stored but not recovered — directly impacting round-trip efficiency and usable capacity.

🌡️ Thermal Stability

Each phase has distinct thermal properties. Spinel phases offer superior thermal stability (+50°C compared to layered), while rock-salt degradation causes irreversible capacity loss. Real-time monitoring of phase composition enables early failure prediction.

Physics Connections

🌌 Cosmological Analog

Phase separation in battery materials follows the same Cahn-Hilliard equation as cosmological phase separation in the early universe. The spinodal decomposition timescale τ ∝ ξ³/D mirrors cooling timescales in gravitational collapse. Phase transitions are universal phenomena across scales.

🔗 Critical Phenomena

Near the critical point, batteries exhibit divergent correlation lengths ξ → ∞ and specific heat anomalies C_v ∝ |T-T_c|^(-α). These critical exponents (α, β, γ, δ) follow scaling laws that mirror second-order phase transitions in condensed matter physics and cosmological inflation.

⚖️ Landau Theory

The Landau free energy expansion F = F₀ + α·φ² + β·φ⁴ + ... describes both battery phase transitions and gravitational phase transitions. The order parameter φ (e.g., magnetization or density) parallels the gravitational order parameter in symmetry breaking during cosmic inflation.
