Stage 1: SEI Formation (0-3 months)

Solid Electrolyte Interphase forms on anode. Initial capacity loss of 2-5%. Irreversible but passivates surface.

95-98%

Stage 2: Stable Operation (3 months - 2 years)

SEI layer stabilizes. Slow calendar aging dominates. Minimal capacity fade under moderate conditions.

90-95%

Stage 3: Transition (2-5 years)

SEI thickens, micro-cracks form. Cycle aging accelerates. Cathode cracks begin. Lithium plating risk increases.

80-90%

Stage 4: Accelerated Aging (5-8 years)

Active material loss, resistance growth, dendritic growth. Capacity fades rapidly. Cell imbalance emerges.

70-80%

Stage 5: End of Life (>8 years)

Capacity below 80% threshold. Internal resistance increased >20%. Safety concerns limit further operation.

<80%
