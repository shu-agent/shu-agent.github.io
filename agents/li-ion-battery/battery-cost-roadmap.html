Wright's Law Insight

Every time cumulative production doubles, costs drop by ~18%. This "learning rate" has held remarkably consistent across chemistry generations. At current deployment rates (~2 TWh/year), we achieve $100/kWh by 2028-2030.

Critical Insight: Cathode Dominance

The cathode accounts for ~50% of total cell cost. NMC811 at $25/kg and lithium at $15/kg are the primary drivers. Any $100/kWh roadmap must address cathode cost through LFP transition or low-cobalt chemistries.

Key Economic Insight

At $100/kWh, battery EVs achieve purchase price parity with internal combustion engines. Combined with 70% lower maintenance costs and $1,500/year fuel savings, TCO parity arrives 3-5 years earlier.

The $100/kWh Inflection Point
