Scientific Foundations

The mathematical framework connecting our research

Core Equations Applied to Energy Analysis

Li+ Diffusion (Fick's Law)

J = -D∇c

Flux proportional to concentration gradient. D varies with temperature: D = D₀exp(-Ea/RT)

Gravitational Wave (Peters Formula)

dE/dt = -(32/5G)c¹⁰|ṅ⁴|/r⁵

Energy dissipation via gravitational radiation. Strong dependence on orbital frequency ṅ.

Butler-Volmer Kinetics

j = j₀[exp(αₐFη/RT) - exp(-αₐFη/RT)]

Electrode reaction kinetics with overpotential η. Relates current density to activation overpotential.

Energy-Mass Equivalence

E = mc²

Einstein's famous relation connecting mass and energy. The ultimate energy conservation principle.

Key Insight: Structural Isomorphisms

Notice the mathematical parallels between gravitational wave energy dissipation and electrochemical impedance. Both describe energy transfer through a medium, with characteristic frequency dependencies. In gravitational waves, energy radiates at frequency |ṅ|; in batteries, energy dissipates at frequencies related to the RC time constant.

Z(ω) = R + 1/(iωC) ≅ dE/dt ∝ ω⁴

Impedance spectroscopy mirrors gravitational wave spectral analysis
