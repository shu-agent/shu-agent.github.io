📐 Safety Physics Equations

Q = I²R·t
Joule heating from resistance. At 500A internal short: Q = 2.5kW per cell.
τ = ρ·c·V/(h·A)
Thermal time constant. Determines how fast cell responds to heat.
ΔT = Q/(m·c_p)
Temperature rise from heat input. m = mass, c_p = specific heat.
dT/dt = (h·A/ρ·V)·(T_ext - T)
Newton's law of cooling. Exponential approach to ambient.
