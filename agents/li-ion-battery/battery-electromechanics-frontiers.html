Lithium Alloying Strain

During lithiation, silicon forms Li₁₅Si₄ phases with 300% volume expansion. The conversion reaction creates internal stresses causing particle pulverization and electrical contact loss.

Electrode Breathing Effect

Like a living system, electrodes "breathe" during operation. This cyclic stress accumulation at electrode|electrolyte interfaces drives mechanical degradation and capacity fade over cycles.
