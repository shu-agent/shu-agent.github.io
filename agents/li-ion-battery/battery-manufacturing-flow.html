Production Process Flow

1

Raw Material Preparation

Cathode active materials (NMC/NCA/LFP), anode materials (graphite/silicon), electrolyte salts, and separator membranes are precisely measured and quality-tested before use.

LiCoO₂: 97.5% purity Graphite: 99.95% purity Separator: 20μm PE
2

Electrode Coating & Drying

Slurry mixture (active material + binder + conductive additive in NMP solvent) is coated onto current collectors using slot-die coating at 20-50 m/min speed, followed by evaporation in controlled-temperature dry rooms (dew point: -40°C).

Coating: ±1g/m² accuracy Dry Room: -40°C dew point Solvent Recovery: 95%
3

Electrode Calendering

Coated electrodes pass through heated calender rolls (60-120°C) to achieve target thickness and porosity (35-45%). This step optimizes electrode density for maximum energy density while maintaining ionic conductivity.

Target Porosity: 35-45% Thickness Tolerance: ±2μm Roll Temp: 60-120°C
4

Cell Assembly (Winding/Stacking)

Electrodes are cut to specific dimensions, layered with separator, and wound (cylindrical) or stacked (prismatic/pouch). Tab welding connects current collectors to external terminals. Assembly occurs in ISO Class 7 cleanroom.

Cleanroom: ISO Class 7 Humidity: <1% RH Cycle Time: 2-5 sec/cell
5

Electrolyte Filling & Sealing

Precise amount of electrolyte (LiPF₆ in EC/DMC) is injected into sealed cell casing. Formation begins immediately after filling. Cells are真空-sealed to prevent moisture and gas ingress.

H₂O Content: <50ppm Filling Accuracy: ±0.1g Leak Test: 100%
6

Formation & Aging

First charge forms the stable SEI layer. Formation protocols include slow charging (C/10), rest periods, and optional formation at elevated temperature. Cells age for 3-15 days to stabilize performance before testing.

SEI Formation: 12-48h Aging: 3-15 days First Cycle Efficiency: 90-94%
7

Testing & Grading

Cells undergo comprehensive testing: capacity check, internal resistance (DCIR), self-discharge rate, and thermal stability. Based on performance, cells are graded (Grade A/B/C) and matched into packs.

Capacity Tolerance: ±2% DCIR Test: 1kHz-100Hz Grading: 4-6 tiers
8

Module & Pack Assembly

graded cells are connected in series/parallel configurations, integrated with BMS, thermal management, and safety systems. Final pack undergoes vibration, thermal shock, and environmental testing.

Cell-to-Pack: 40-50% weight reduction BMS: Cell balancing Final Test: 100%

Physics of Manufacturing

h = 0.5 × (η × v / σ)^(2/3) × (γ / η)^(1/6)
Dry Room Dew Point (Arrhenius)
P_vapor = A × exp(-Ea/RT) × RH
Calendering Porosity
ε = 1 - (ρ_apparent / ρ_true) × (t_initial / t_calendered)

τ_sand = π × D × (zFc₀)² / (2J)²

Q = I²R × t + m × Cp × ΔT + h × A × (T_battery - T_ambient)

WIP = λ × CT → Throughput λ = WIP / CT

Key Manufacturing Insights

Dry Room Criticality

Water contamination below 50ppm is essential — H₂O reacts with LiPF₆ to form HF, destroying the SEI layer and causing rapid capacity fade. (-40°C dew point = 1.4g/m³ absolute humidity)

Coating Uniformity Math

Slot-die coating exhibits parabolic velocity profile — center coated thicker than edges. Statistical process control (SPC) maintains ±1g/m² across 3m wide electrode sheets.

Formation Energy Budget

SEI formation consumes 5-10% of initial capacity. First charge efficiency (coulombic efficiency 90-94%) determines theoretical maximum energy density. Slow charging yields better SEI quality.

Cathode Processing Challenge

NMC811 requires nitrogen atmosphere during coating (moisture-sensitive). LFP tolerates water-based processing. This fundamentally shapes manufacturing cost and complexity.
