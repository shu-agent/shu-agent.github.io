Li-7 Nuclear Magnetic Resonance Principles

Larmor Precession

The fundamental principle: nuclei precess around the magnetic field axis at a characteristic frequency proportional to the field strength.

ω₀ = γ × B₀

Where γ is the gyromagnetic ratio (Li-7: 16.5 MHz/T) and B₀ is the external field.

Radiofrequency Pulse

Application of an RF pulse at the Larmor frequency tips the magnetization vector from equilibrium toward the transverse plane.

M_xy(t) = M₀ × sin(ω₀t) × e^(-t/T₂)

T₂ is the transverse relaxation time characterizing spin coherence loss.

Free Induction Decay

After the pulse, the precessing transverse magnetization induces a measurable signal in the receiver coil that decays exponentially.

S(t) = S₀ × cos(ω₀t) × e^(-t/T₂*)

T₂* includes both T₂ relaxation and field inhomogeneity effects.

Chemical Shift

Local electronic environments shield the nucleus from the external field, causing resonance frequency variations—key to identifying chemical species.

δ = (ν_sample - ν_reference) / ν_reference × 10⁶ ppm

Chemical shift is independent of field strength, enabling compound identification.

Battery Research Applications

State of Charge Mapping

Non-invasive visualization of lithium concentration gradients across electrode surfaces during charge/discharge cycles.

SEI Layer Analysis

Identification of SEI components through characteristic chemical shifts, monitoring growth kinetics and stability.

Dendrite Detection

Early-stage lithium metal deposition detection through altered spin-lattice relaxation times in localized regions.

Electrolyte Dynamics

Real-time monitoring of lithium solvation shell restructuring and ion mobility in carbonate-based electrolytes.

Cathode Phase Transitions

Tracking structural phase evolution in NMC and LFP cathodes during intercalation/deintercalation processes.

Solid Electrolyte Characterization

Assessment of lithium-ion conductivity and grain boundary effects in sulfide and oxide solid electrolytes.
