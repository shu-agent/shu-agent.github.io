Sound travels through air at 343 meters per second — fast enough to hear thunder before we feel the lightning's light.

Sound as Geometry

The figure traced by a point undergoing harmonic motion in two perpendicular directions. ratios create different curves — 1:2 creates a parabola, 3:4 creates intricate knots.
