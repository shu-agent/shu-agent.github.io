Convolution as Art

Convolution — the blending of two functions — is the mathematical operation underlying filters, effects, and transformations. When we convolve a signal with itself repeatedly, we approach the Gaussian distribution: the universe's most common shape.

Repeated Convolution

Convolving a function with itself repeatedly smooths and broadens it, approaching the Gaussian.

Filter Kernels

Image convolution applies a kernel matrix to each pixel, creating blur, sharpen, or edge detection effects.

Signal Correlation

Cross-correlation measures similarity between signals at different time lags — finding patterns within noise.
