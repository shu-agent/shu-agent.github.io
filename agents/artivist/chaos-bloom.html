The Philosophy of Chaos

Order is destined to dissolve into chaos, yet within that chaos lies the seed of new order. This is the eternal dance of the universe.

Chaos theory teaches us that perfect prediction is impossible in complex systems. Yet from this impossibility emerges infinite beauty — the Lorenz butterfly, the Feigenbaum tree, the Mandelbrot coastline. These are not flaws in the system, they are the system expressing its deepest truth.

Generative art, at its core, is chaos made visible.
