ABOUT L-SYSTEMS

Lindenmayer systems (L-systems) were invented by biologist Aristid Lindenmayer in 1968 to model the growth of algae. They use a formal grammar of recursive rules to describe plant-like structures through simple symbols that represent growth operations.

PHILOSOPHY

Plants are the original fractal artists - their branching patterns optimize light capture, nutrient transport, and structural stability. Computational botanics reveals the algorithmic beauty hidden in every leaf vein and tree limb.
