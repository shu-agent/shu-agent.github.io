The Physics of Sound & Light

Fourier Transform

Any complex waveform decomposes into simple sine waves. This is the mathematical foundation of audio visualization — we reverse this process to transform frequency data back into visual form.

F(ω) = ∫f(t)e^(-iωt)dt

Resonance & Harmonics

Every object has natural resonant frequencies. The harmonics that emerge follow the harmonic series: f, 2f, 3f, 4f... These same patterns appear in atomic spectra, planetary orbits, and musical scales.

f_n = n · f_1

Wave-Particle Duality

Sound waves create standing wave patterns that behave like particles. In speaker systems, these "phonons" interact with physical space the same way photons interact with optical systems.

λ = v / f

Beat Frequencies

When two similar frequencies interfere, they create beats. The beat frequency equals their difference. This principle underlies noise-canceling technology and quantum interference patterns.

f_beat = |f_1 - f_2|
