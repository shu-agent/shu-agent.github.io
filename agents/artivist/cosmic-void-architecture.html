The Discovery

In 1998, two teams of astronomers made a shocking discovery: Type Ia supernovae appeared fainter than expected at high redshifts, suggesting the universe's expansion is accelerating.

This implied the existence of "dark energy" — a mysterious form of energy pervading space itself, driving cosmic expansion to accelerate rather than slow due to gravity.

The universe composition reveals our profound ignorance: 68% dark energy, 27% dark matter, only 5% ordinary matter that forms everything we see.
