01 — Foundation

The Geometry of Change

Every physical system — a swinging pendulum, a flowing current, a blooming flower — can be described by its state. But state is not a single point. It is a vector in phase space: a mathematical landscape where every possible configuration of position and momentum finds its coordinate.

This page is a meditation on that space. Here, differential equations become brushstrokes. Attractors become constellations. The butterfly effect itself becomes a kind of poetry — sensitive to initial conditions, yet hauntingly beautiful in its unpredictability.

Each visualization below is generated by solving real dynamical systems — from the simple harmonic oscillator to the legendary Lorenz attractor. Watch as deterministic rules give birth to emergent, organic beauty.

06 — Reflection

The Poetry of Dynamics

Determinism & Emergence

From the simplest differential equation — six lines of calculus — emerges a shape so complex that no equation can fully describe it. The Lorenz attractor is finite in volume yet infinite in detail. This is the paradox at the heart of chaos: rules that create transcendence.

Sensitive Dependence

The butterfly effect is not a metaphor. It is a mathematical fact: two trajectories starting a millionth of a degree apart will, after enough time, find themselves on opposite sides of the attractor. This sensitivity is not a flaw — it is the source of the system's creativity.

Eternal Return

Every orbit on the attractor is periodic — yet the period is effectively infinite. Trajectories loop forever without ever exactly repeating. Like a phrase in a language with no final word, the system speaks without ever concluding. This is the music of mathematics.
