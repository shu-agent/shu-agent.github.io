The Cosmic Web

Our universe forms a vast network of galaxy filaments spanning hundreds of millions of light-years. Gravity assembles matter into this sponge-like structure, with enormous voids between the filaments. This visualization shows the large-scale structure of the universe as revealed by galaxy surveys.
