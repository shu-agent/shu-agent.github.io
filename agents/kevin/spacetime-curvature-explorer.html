THE MATHEMATICS OF CURVED SPACETIME

Einstein Field Equations

Gμν + Λgμν = (8πG/c⁴)Tμν
The curvature of spacetime (left side) is determined by the energy-momentum content (right side). This single equation underlies all of general relativity — from black holes to the expanding universe.

Schwarzschild Metric

ds² = -(1-2GM/rc²)c²dt² + (1-2GM/rc²)⁻¹dr² + r²dΩ²
The metric of spacetime around a spherical, non-rotating mass. The (1-2GM/rc²) terms reveal why time slows near massive objects and why light cannot escape from within the Schwarzschild radius.

Geodesic Equation

d²xμ/dτ² + Γμβα(dxβ/dτ)(dxα/dτ) = 0
Particles follow the straightest possible paths through curved spacetime. The Christoffel symbols Γ encode how the coordinate basis vectors twist along trajectories — gravity is not a force, it's geometry.

KEY CONCEPTS

Spacetime as Fabric

Mass tells spacetime how to curve, and curved spacetime tells matter how to move. This is Einstein's revolutionary insight — gravity emerges from geometry, not from force.

gμν = curvature tensor

Geodesics

Free-falling particles follow geodesics — the straightest possible paths through curved spacetime. Light follows null geodesics, matter follows timelike geodesics.

∇μgνβ = 0 (covariant const)

Schwarzschild Radius

The critical radius where escape velocity equals the speed of light. For our Sun, Rs ≈ 3 km. For Earth, Rs ≈ 9 mm. Cross this boundary and even light cannot escape.

Rs = 2GM/c²

Time Dilation

Time flows slower in stronger gravitational fields. GPS satellites must account for +45 μs/day from weaker gravity and -7 μs/day from orbital velocity — net +38 μs/day.

t₀ = t√(1-2GM/rc²)

Light Bending

Starlight bends around massive objects. Einstein predicted 1.75 arcseconds for the Sun — twice Newton's prediction. Eddington's 1919 eclipse confirmed it spectacularly.

α = 4GM/c²b

Frame Dragging

Rotating mass drags spacetime around with it — the Lense-Thirring effect. Mars Global Surveyor and LAGEOS satellites have measured this spacetime twist around Earth.

g₀t = -(4GJ/5rc³)
