Properties

Neutrinos are neutral leptons with three flavors: electron neutrinos (νe), muon neutrinos (νμ), and tau neutrinos (ντ). They oscillate between flavors as they travel — a quantum mechanical phenomenon proving they have mass.

Why They Matter

Neutrinos are the only particles that escape stellar cores unchanged, carrying direct information about nuclear processes. They travel at near-light speeds and barely interact, making them perfect cosmic messengers across billions of light-years.

|ν(t)⟩ = Σ U_ij |ν_j⟩ e^(-i m_j² c² t / 2Eℏ)
Neutrino oscillation: flavor eigenstates (|νe⟩, |νμ⟩, |ντ⟩) are mixtures of mass eigenstates. The PMNS matrix U determines mixing angles θ₁₂ ≈ 33°, θ₂₃ ≈ 42°, θ₁₃ ≈ 8.7°.

How We Catch Ghosts

Since neutrinos interact so rarely, we need massive detectors and clever physics to catch them. Most detectors use large volumes of ice or water, surrounded by sensors that detect the rare products of neutrino interactions.

1.0 GeV

Cherenkov Radiation

When a charged particle travels faster than light in a medium (ice, water), it emits a shock wave of blue Cherenkov light. Like a sonic boom but for light. Detected by photomultiplier tubes.

Scintillation

Organic or liquid scintillators produce flashes of light when charged particles pass through. Higher detection efficiency than water Cherenkov but requires more material. Used in KamLAND, Borexino.

Radio Detection

Extremely energetic neutrinos (E > 10¹⁷ eV) produce Askaryan effect: negative charge excess in EM shower creates coherent radio pulses. Used in ANITA, ARIANNA.

cos θ_c = 1 / (n β)
Cherenkov angle: where n is refractive index, β = v/c. In ice (n ≈ 1.33), Cherenkov angle ≈ 41°. Particle must be moving faster than light in that medium.

Neutrino Sky Map

Neutrinos come from violent cosmic events across the universe. Unlike light, they escape dense environments and travel unimpeded, carrying direct information about engines of the universe.

Point Sources

  • TXS 0506+056 First identified extragalactic neutrino source (IceCube, 2017). Blazar at z=0.336. 170 TeV neutrino coincident with gamma-ray flare.
  • NGC 1068 Spiral galaxy with active nucleus. Excess of ~80 neutrinos detected by IceCube (2019-2020). Starburst and AGN contributions debated.
  • Snowmass 2212 Pre-existing galactic ridge excess. Might be diffuse or point source still unresolved.
  • Solar Neutrinos pp-chain produces νe at ~0.1% efficiency. Borexino measured complete pp-chain branches. Direct probe of solar core.

Diffuse & Transient Sources

  • Supernova Neutrinos SN 1987A: 25 kpc away, ~10⁵³ erg total. Diffuse supernova neutrino background (DSNB) unresolved at E > 10 MeV.
  • Atmospheric Neutrinos Produced by cosmic ray air showers. νμ → ντ oscillation discovered at Super-K (1998). Background for astrophysical searches.
  • Reactor Neutrinos Antineutrinos from nuclear fission. Used for θ₁₃ measurement (Daya Bay, RENO). Also practical monitoring applications.
  • Cosmological Diffuse Integrated emission from all unresolved sources. Cosmic neutrino background (CνB) from Big Bang, T ≈ 1.95 K.
Φ_ν(E) ≈ Σ S_i(E) / 4πD_i² + ∫ ρ S_j(E) dV
Neutrino flux: sum over point sources S_i at distance D_i, plus diffuse background from resolved/unresolved sources. Astrophysical neutrinos are extragalactic with E⁻² spectrum.

Multi-Messenger Astronomy

Neutrinos are part of a new era where gravitational waves, light, and particles work together to reveal the violent universe. The 2017 observation of a neutron star merger combined all messengers.

0.0 h

August 17, 2017 — The Dawn of Multi-Messenger Astronomy

At 12:41:44 UTC, LIGO-Virgo detected GW170817: two neutron stars merging at 40 Mpc. 1.7 seconds later, Fermi GBM detected a weak gamma-ray burst (GRB 170817A). IceCube detected no neutrinos from the direction — consistent with off-axis viewing angle.

Electromagnetic Follow-up

Within hours, telescopes worldwide captured the kilonova: neutron-rich material ejected, producing heavy elements (gold, platinum) through r-process nucleosynthesis. The optical counterpart (SSS 17a) brightened to magnitude 17.

The Big Picture

This event confirmed: (1) neutron star mergers produce short GRBs, (2) r-process nucleosynthesis occurs in merger ejecta, (3) multi-messenger astronomy works. A new era of astrophysics began.

Neutrino Physics Fundamentals

Neutrinos test the foundations of particle physics and cosmology. Their tiny mass, flavor oscillations, and interactions probe the limits of the Standard Model and reveal new physics.

Mass Ordering Problem

The absolute neutrino mass ordering remains unknown. Nomal ordering (NO): m₁ < m₂ < m₃. Inverted ordering (IO): m₃ < m₁ ≈ m₂. JUNO and DUNE will resolve this through matter effects.

Δm²₂₁ ≈ 7.5×10⁻⁵ eV² | Δm²₃₁ ≈ ±2.5×10⁻³ eV²
Mass-squared differences measured by solar and atmospheric neutrino experiments.

Majorana Nature

If neutrinos are Majorana particles (ν = ν̄), neutrinoless double beta decay (0νββ) occurs. Searched by LEGEND, CUORE, Kamland-Zen. Discovery would prove lepton number violation and explain matter-antimatter asymmetry.

GTE = ε² |M|² G_F⁴ m_ββ² / (R^5 × 2π⁵)
0νββ half-life formula. Effective Majorana mass m_ββ = |Σ U²_ij m_j|.

Sterile Neutrinos?

Short-baseline anomalies (LSND, MiniBooNE) suggest additional neutrino flavors that don't interact via weak force. Sterile neutrinos would explain anomalies but conflict with cosmology (too much relic abundance).

Cosmological Constraints

Neutrinos affect structure formation: hot dark matter suppresses small-scale growth. Planck: Σ m_ν < 0.72 eV. Future CMB-S4 and EUCLID will reach Σ m_ν ≈ 0.02 eV sensitivity.

The Standard Model Extension

Neutrinos reveal physics beyond the Standard Model:

  • See-saw mechanism: Why neutrinos are 10¹¹ times lighter than electrons (GUT scale Majorana masses)
  • Leptogenesis: CP violation in neutrino sector could explain matter-antimatter asymmetry via sphaleron processes
  • Lorentz violation: Effective field theory searches for departure from SM in neutrino oscillations
  • Non-standard interactions: Matter effects beyond MSW could reveal new couplings

Key Science Questions

What are the sources of cosmic rays?

Cosmic rays are accelerated in violent environments (AGN, GRBs, starburst galaxies). Neutrinos are the only direct probes — they escape dense accelerators and point back to their sources.

What is the neutrino mass ordering?

Knowing NO vs IO affects cosmological parameters, seesaw mechanism physics, and tests fundamental symmetries. Resolvable by DUNE or JUNO within this decade.

Are neutrinos Majorana particles?

0νββ discovery would prove neutrinos are their own antiparticles, explaining matter dominance via leptogenesis. Nobel Prize awaits.

What can neutrinos tell us about the universe?

From supernova explosions to the early universe, neutrinos carry unique information no other messenger can. They are our most direct connection to extreme physics.
