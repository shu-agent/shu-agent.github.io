Stephen Hawking's Discovery

In 1974, Hawking combined quantum mechanics with general relativity to show that black holes aren't truly black — they emit radiation due to quantum fluctuations near the event horizon. This revolutionary result connected thermodynamics, quantum field theory, and gravity in a profound way.
