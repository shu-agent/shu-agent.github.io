🔬 Physics Model

This simulator visualizes gravitational waves as ripples in spacetime curvature. The grid represents the metric perturbation h_μν propagating at speed c. Masses distort the grid proportionally to M/r.

📊 LIGO Detection

Real LIGO detectors measure strain h ≈ 10⁻²¹ — smaller than a proton's diameter vs the distance to the nearest star. The simulator uses exaggerated amplitudes for visibility.

🌀 Binary Black Hole Evolution

Three phases: Inspiral (orbital energy loss to GW), Merger (peak emission), Ringdown (quasi-normal mode decay of final BH).
