Hubble's Law

The foundational discovery that revealed our expanding universe. Edwin Hubble's 1929 observations showed that galaxies recede from us with velocity proportional to their distance — the signature of an expanding cosmos.

Cosmological Redshift

Light from distant galaxies is stretched by the expansion of spacetime itself — not by Doppler motion through space. This redshift z directly measures how much the universe has expanded since the light was emitted.

Key Realizations

Great Filter: Something prevents civilizations from becoming interstellar. Could be behind us (hard steps to life) or ahead (self-destruction, isolation).

SETI: Search for Extraterrestrial Intelligence uses radio telescopes to listen for intentional broadcasts from other civilizations.

Timeline: Voyager spacecraft will reach nearby stars in 40,000 years. Human radio signals have traveled only 200 light-years.
