PHASE 03A

RED GIANT / SUPERGIANT

When core hydrogen exhausts, the star expands dramatically. Hydrogen shell burning causes the outer layers to swell, cooling the surface to red. For massive stars, it becomes a red supergiant like Betelgeuse.

PHASE 03B

SUPERNOVA

Iron core collapse triggers an apocalyptic explosion. In 10 seconds, the star releases more energy than the Sun will emit in its entire lifetime. Heavier elements beyond iron are forged in the shockwave.

PLANETARY NEBULA

PHASE 04B

NEUTRON STAR / BLACK HOLE

The collapsed core packs the mass of the Sun into a city-sized sphere. Neutron degeneracy pressure halts collapse for masses below 3 M☉. Beyond this limit, nothing can resist gravity — a black hole forms.
